Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

engineerfamily.net is a personal website. You can read every page without signing in.

Signing in with Google

If you choose to sign in with Google, engineerfamily.net keeps a sign-in cookie in your browser for up to 30 days. It holds your Google account ID, your email address and your name, and nothing else. No password, no Google access token and no other Google data is kept. The cookie is signed so it can't be changed, and it is only sent to this website.

Signing out deletes the cookie. Signing in on its own gives you no extra access to anything.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared with anyone or used for advertising.

Strelitzia

The Strelitzia job dashboard at /strelitzia/dashboard/ and its optional Google Drive access have their own policy: the Strelitzia privacy policy.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on GitHub.
