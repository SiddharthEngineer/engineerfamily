Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

ApplyPilot is open-source software that you install and run yourself, on your own computer or server. There is no ApplyPilot account and no ApplyPilot server. The people who make ApplyPilot don't receive, store or see your data.

Data ApplyPilot handles

  • Your profile and resume, which you provide during setup, stored in files on your machine.
  • Job postings collected from public job boards and career pages, stored in a database on your machine.
  • Generated documents: tailored resumes and cover letters, created on your machine.

To score postings and write documents, ApplyPilot sends your resume text and the job description to the AI model provider you configure (for example Google Gemini). That provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to those requests.

Google user data

If you connect Google Drive, ApplyPilot asks for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This permission only covers files and folders that ApplyPilot creates. ApplyPilot can't see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

ApplyPilot uses this access only to:

  • create folders named after the company, role and date of a job application;
  • upload your tailored resume and cover letter PDFs into those folders, and update them when you regenerate them;
  • download those same PDFs back to your machine when you apply to that job;
  • record each file's Drive link in the database on your machine, so you can find it later.

Your Google sign-in token is saved only on your machine, in ApplyPilot's settings folder, readable only by your user account. Google Drive data is never sent to the ApplyPilot developers or to anyone else, never sold, and never used for advertising or to train AI models.

ApplyPilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Revoke ApplyPilot's Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete ~/.applypilot/google_token.json.
  • Files ApplyPilot put in your Drive are yours. Delete them in Google Drive like any other file.
  • Delete the ~/.applypilot folder to remove everything ApplyPilot stored on your machine.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the project's GitHub repository. Questions can be raised as a GitHub issue.
