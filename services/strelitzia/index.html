Your data stays yours

Strelitzia is self-hosted: it runs on your own computer or server, not as a hosted service, and your resume and job data stay there.

Google Drive

If you turn it on, Strelitzia saves your tailored resumes and cover letters to your own Google Drive, in folders by company, role and date, so you can open them from any device. It asks for the drive.file permission, which only lets it see and manage the files and folders Strelitzia itself created. It can't read anything else in your Drive. See the privacy policy for details.
