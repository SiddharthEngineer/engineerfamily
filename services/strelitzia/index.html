A self-hosted job search assistant

Strelitzia finds job postings, scores how well they fit your background, and writes a tailored resume and cover letter for each one. It runs on your own computer or server, not as a hosted service.

What it does

  • Collects postings from public job boards and company career pages.
  • Scores each posting against your resume and preferences.
  • Writes a one-page tailored resume and a cover letter as PDFs.
  • Gives you a dashboard to track the applications you send yourself. It never submits anything for you.

Google Drive

If you turn it on, Strelitzia saves your tailored resumes and cover letters to your own Google Drive, in folders by company, role and date, so you can open them from any device. It asks for the drive.file permission, which only lets it see and manage the files and folders Strelitzia itself created. It can't read anything else in your Drive. See the privacy policy for details.

Get it

Strelitzia is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license (it began as a fork of ApplyPilot). The source code, setup guide and issue tracker are on GitHub.
